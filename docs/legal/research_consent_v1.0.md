# PART B: RESEARCH AND AI MODEL IMPROVEMENT CONSENT (OPTIONAL)

## Plain Language Summary

We are asking your permission to use the plate photographs and related data you generate while using Plate-Clean Rewards beyond what is needed to run the service. Specifically, we want to:

- train and improve the AI vision model that estimates how much food you consumed;
- conduct research on food waste reduction, restaurant operations, and dietary patterns; and
- publish findings in academic papers, industry reports, and open datasets that support the wider goal of reducing food waste and building sustainable food systems in India and globally.

**This consent is entirely voluntary. You can use Plate-Clean Rewards fully without giving it, and you can withdraw it at any time.**

If you give consent and later change your mind, we stop using your future data for these purposes. We cannot always retrieve data that has already been incorporated into a trained model or a published research dataset (see Section 6 below).

If you are comfortable with the above, tick the consent box. If you are not sure, please read the full text that follows.

## 1. Introduction

1.1. This part (the "Research Consent") describes how the Company proposes to process your personal data for research and AI model improvement purposes, and seeks your specific, informed, and voluntary consent under Section 6 of the DPDP Act.

1.2. This Research Consent is separate from and additional to Part A of these Terms and to our Privacy Policy. Your acceptance of Part A does not, by itself, constitute consent to the processing described here.

1.3. Giving this consent is entirely optional and has no effect on your ability to use Plate-Clean Rewards.

## 2. What Data is Covered

2.1. If you provide this consent, the following data may be processed for the purposes set out in Section 3:

  (a) **Plate photographs**, being the "before" and "after" images you submit during each meal session;

  (b) **Consumption estimates** generated by the AI model, and any staff-adjusted or staff-approved final scores;

  (c) **Order records** for the associated meal session, including items ordered, portion sizes, and meal category;

  (d) **Timestamps and coarse restaurant identifiers** such as city-level and cuisine-type identifiers, but not table-level or diner-level identifiers;

  (e) **Anonymised session metadata** such as time between capture and validation, decision latency, and model confidence scores.

2.2. The following data is not included in this Research Consent and is not used for research or AI training:

  (a) your name, mobile number, email address, or any other direct identifier;

  (b) your device fingerprint or IP address;

  (c) your precise location (GPS coordinates);

  (d) any payment or financial data.

2.3. **De-identification.** Before any data is used for research or AI training, it is processed to remove or hash direct identifiers linking it to you. Direct identifiers are not stored alongside research datasets.

## 3. Purposes

3.1. Data covered by Clause 2.1 may be used for the following purposes only:

  (a) **AI model improvement.** Training, evaluating, and fine-tuning computer vision models that estimate food consumption, dish recognition, portion estimation, and related tasks, including training successor models to replace the current hosted vision LLM in production.

  (b) **Academic and applied research.** Analysing patterns of restaurant food waste, evaluating the effectiveness of behavioural interventions, and generating insights for food sustainability research.

  (c) **Publication.** Publishing aggregated findings, methodology, and evaluation results in academic journals, conference proceedings, industry white papers, and comparable venues.

  (d) **Public interest datasets.** Contributing curated, fully anonymised subsets to research communities or the public, where doing so advances the field of food waste reduction, and only after further review to remove any residual re-identification risk.

  (e) **Model benchmarking and reproducibility.** Sharing evaluation datasets with academic collaborators or independent researchers under appropriate data use agreements that impose matching confidentiality obligations.

3.2. **Purpose limitation.** Data will not be used for:

  (a) advertising or marketing to you or any third party;

  (b) individual profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects;

  (c) sale of your personal data to any third party;

  (d) any purpose not disclosed in this Research Consent.

## 4. Legal Basis

4.1. The legal basis for the processing described in this Research Consent is your specific and informed consent under Section 6 of the DPDP Act.

4.2. You may exercise all rights available to you as a "Data Principal" under the DPDP Act, including the right to withdraw this consent at any time.

## 5. Who Accesses the Data

5.1. **Company personnel.** Data science and engineering personnel of the Company, bound by written confidentiality obligations, will access the data for the purposes in Section 3.

5.2. **Academic and research collaborators.** From time to time the Company may share fully de-identified data with academic or research institutions under a Data Use Agreement that:

  (a) restricts use to the purposes in Section 3;

  (b) prohibits re-identification;

  (c) requires deletion of the shared data at the end of the specified project;

  (d) requires equivalent confidentiality safeguards.

5.3. **Cloud service providers.** Data storage and processing may occur on the infrastructure of the Company's cloud service providers, currently AWS in the Mumbai region, under written data processing agreements.

5.4. **No unrelated third parties.** Data covered by this Research Consent is not sold, licensed for commercial use, or otherwise made available to any party outside the categories in Clauses 5.1 to 5.3.

## 6. Withdrawal of Consent

6.1. You may withdraw this Research Consent at any time, without giving any reason, by:

  (a) toggling the "Research and AI improvement consent" option to OFF in the Plate-Clean Rewards account settings; or

  (b) writing to research@hypergeometriclabs.com or grievance@hypergeometriclabs.com.

6.2. **Effect of withdrawal.**

  (a) **Going forward.** On withdrawal, no data generated by you after the withdrawal date will be used for the purposes in Section 3. Any operational retention of your plate photographs will revert to the default seven (7) day period described in the Privacy Policy.

  (b) **Historic data.** On withdrawal, the Company will remove your de-identified historic data from its active research and training datasets within sixty (60) days.

  (c) **Trained models.** It is not technically or scientifically feasible to remove your contribution from a model that has already been trained on your data. Data that has been used to train a model prior to your withdrawal may continue to influence that model's behaviour. The Company will not use your data to train new models after your withdrawal.

  (d) **Published datasets and papers.** Where your de-identified data has already been incorporated into a released dataset or published research, the Company cannot retract or amend the publication or dataset. Because releases are always de-identified and aggregated, this does not identify you.

6.3. Withdrawing this Research Consent does not affect your account, your ability to earn Rewards, or your rights under Part A of these Terms.

## 7. Retention

7.1. Data processed under this Research Consent is retained for the period reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes in Section 3.

7.2. **Operational retention** of plate photographs while you have consent active: up to ninety (90) days by default. This is longer than the seven (7) day operational default under Part A, and this longer period applies only for the duration of your active consent.

7.3. **Training dataset retention.** De-identified data included in a research or training dataset may be retained for as long as that dataset is in active use for the purposes in Section 3, subject to the withdrawal rights in Section 6.

7.4. **Publication artefacts.** Aggregated statistics and de-identified samples included in a published paper or released dataset are retained indefinitely as part of the scientific record.

## 8. Security

8.1. The Company implements reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data covered by this Research Consent, including:

  (a) encryption at rest and in transit;

  (b) access controls limiting research data access to authorised personnel;

  (c) audit logging of access to research datasets;

  (d) de-identification and hashing of direct identifiers before inclusion in research datasets;

  (e) written confidentiality and data use agreements with all persons or institutions who receive shared data.

## 9. International Transfers

9.1. Data processed under this Research Consent is primarily stored and processed within India.

9.2. Where research collaboration requires the sharing of de-identified data with an academic institution located outside India, such sharing will be undertaken only:

  (a) in accordance with the DPDP Act and any rules issued thereunder;

  (b) under a written Data Use Agreement providing equivalent safeguards;

  (c) with data that has been de-identified in accordance with Clause 2.3.

## 10. Impact and Ethics Commitments

10.1. The Company undertakes that research and AI improvement activities under this Research Consent will:

  (a) prioritise the reduction of food waste and the improvement of restaurant sustainability outcomes;

  (b) not be used to develop systems for surveillance, profiling of individuals, or advertising targeting;

  (c) be conducted in accordance with widely recognised research ethics standards, including principles of beneficence, non-maleficence, and respect for persons;

  (d) not encourage overeating, body image harms, or any behaviour contrary to the Company's ethics rules in its operational specifications.

## 11. Your Rights

11.1. In respect of data processed under this Research Consent, you have the rights of a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, including the rights to:

  (a) obtain a summary of the personal data being processed and the processing activities;

  (b) request correction of inaccurate personal data;

  (c) request erasure of your personal data, subject to Clause 6.2;

  (d) withdraw this consent at any time;

  (e) nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity;

  (f) file a grievance with the Company and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

11.2. Requests may be made through the account settings in Plate-Clean Rewards or by writing to grievance@hypergeometriclabs.com.

## 12. Notification of Changes

12.1. If we wish to use data covered by this Research Consent for a purpose materially different from those described in Section 3, we will not do so without first seeking your fresh consent.

12.2. Non-material clarifications and updates will be posted with an updated version number and effective date.

## 13. Governing Law

13.1. This Research Consent is governed by the laws of India and, in particular, by the DPDP Act.

## 14. Consent Declaration

By ticking the "I consent to the use of my data for research and AI model improvement" box, you confirm that:

  (a) you have read and understood this Research Consent;

  (b) you understand that this consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn at any time;

  (c) you understand the categories of data involved, the purposes for which it will be used, and the retention consequences described in Sections 2, 3, and 7;

  (d) you understand the limitations on withdrawal described in Clause 6.2, particularly for data already used to train a model or included in a published dataset;

  (e) you give your specific and informed consent to the processing described in this document.
